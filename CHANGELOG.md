# Changelog

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-15): Spot-light HD

A new, realistic version of Spot-light, in its own repository.

- Spot is a furred, animated black lab: shell fur with a blue sheen, red collar and brass tag,
  IK legs that never slide, walk, trot and gallop, crouch with a butt wiggle, pounce, rear up at
  the wall, sit, sniff, pant, wag, blink, lie down and sleep.
- His brain: grip per floor (he skids on wood and stone, turns sharp on a rug or grass), line of
  sight around furniture, searching when the dot goes out, barking at a dot out of reach,
  boredom, energy and happiness.
- Three places: a living room with a fire, a garden with grass in the wind and weather, and a
  snowy cabin with a Christmas tree. Day, evening and night.
- Seven modes: Dodge, Endless, Lure, Fetch, Zoomies, Hide & seek and Relax. Every game ends with a
  treat for Spot.
- Photo mode with orbit camera, depth of field, five looks and PNG export.
- Synthesised sound: paws per floor type, panting, barks, tag jingle, skids, fire, rain, birds.
- Mouse, touch, keyboard and gamepad; four cameras; quality presets with Auto; the 8-bit look;
  settings, best scores per place, stats and 14 achievements; works offline as a PWA.
- One self-contained index.html, still runs from a double-click. The original game is in classic/.
